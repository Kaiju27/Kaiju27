🗂️ Projects
🍽️ The Pressure Cooker — Uber Eats Audience Research
Brief: Investigate the lived experience of dinnertime among young adults (the "MAD years") and develop a strategic creative brief for Uber Eats.
What I did:
Conducted 6 in-depth qualitative interviews (1–2 hours each), in-person and online
Built empathy maps for each participant using Miro
Identified key behavioural insights around stress, identity, and food habits
Developed a full creative brief positioning Uber Eats as an emotional "release valve" for the 20–30s audience
Key insight: "They're not hungry, they're overwhelmed — and dinner has to fix it."
Skills: Qualitative research · Audience insight · Empathy mapping · Strategic planning · Creative briefing

🛒 Rebranding the Invisible — Tesco Clubcard Strategy
Brief: Identify an underserved audience and develop a brand strategy to reposition Tesco Clubcard beyond its transactional utility.
What I did:
Conducted a full UK loyalty market analysis (Clubcard vs. Nectar, Boots, M&S Sparks)
Identified "Invisible Women" (UK women aged 50–60) as a high-value, emotionally underserved audience
Developed a GET/WHO/TO/BY strategic framework
Proposed a dual-channel campaign (TV + influencer social) with full KPI measurement framework
Key insight: "Once their role as the keystone of the family fades, these women are left feeling invisible — because much of their identity was built around being essential to others."
Skills: Market analysis · Brand strategy · Campaign planning · Measurement frameworks · Audience segmentation
